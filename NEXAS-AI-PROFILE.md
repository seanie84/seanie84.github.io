## What Is Nexas AI?

Nexas AI is a complete AI workforce for a business, delivered through one secure, sci-fi-grade command center. Instead of a single general-purpose chatbot, Nexas AI puts 77 specialised AI agents at your disposal — each with a name, a defined role, a personality and a focused set of capabilities — covering every function a business needs: leadership, legal, sales, marketing, operations, HR, finance, research, technology, security, maintenance, creative work, education, health and more.

At the heart of the platform sits **NEXA**, the head butler and command core. NEXA is your single point of contact: ask it anything about your business and it answers across every domain, manages memory and context, and hands work to the right specialist. Alongside NEXA works **Amber**, the chief orchestrator, who turns a plain-language brief into a structured mission — with owners, steps and a finish line — and coordinates multiple agents to deliver it, then synthesises their output into one answer.

What makes Nexas AI different is that it is grounded in the South African reality. Its specialists speak the language of CIPC registration, SARS, VAT and PAYE, the CCMA, the BCEA and LRA, POPIA, the OHS Act, the CAPS curriculum and TVET colleges. Its advice is priced in Rand, planned around load-shedding, and delivered in plain language — no jargon, no guru nonsense, no pretence.

- **77 specialised AI agents** — a full virtual staff complement, each agent named, titled and scoped to a real business role with defined capabilities.

- **Memory** — long-term context and knowledge base management, so the platform knows your business and doesn't start from zero each session.
- **Classroom** — AI-powered training and learning modules for you and your team.
- **Plugins** — connect external tools and integrations to extend what the workforce can reach.
- **Theme Studio** — customise the look of your command center to suit the operator.
- **Secure operator sign-in** — a private, per-business workspace behind authentication.
- **South African specialisation** — local law, labour, tax, compliance, education and market knowledge as a first-class capability, not an afterthought.
- **Plain-language delivery** — every agent is briefed to explain clearly, honestly and practically, and to refer you to real professionals where that is the right answer.

## How Can Nexas AI Be Used?

Nexas AI works the way a well-run company does: you talk to the front office, and the right people get it done.

1. **Ask NEXA anything.** Sign in and put any business question or task to the command core. NEXA answers directly or brings in the right specialist, and remembers your context between conversations.
2. **Go straight to a specialist.** Open the agent directory, pick the expert for the job — the tax consultant, the labour lawyer, the SEO specialist, the master mechanic — and work with them one-on-one.
3. **Launch a mission for bigger jobs.** When the work spans departments — "launch this product", "fix my cash flow", "prepare us for this dispute" — brief Amber in Mission Control. She breaks it into steps, assigns each to the right agents and reports back with one synthesised result.
4. **Monitor everything from the War Room.** Watch live agent operations in the active operations centre, and track the big picture — agents, missions, contacts, analytics — from your dashboard.
5. **Keep the outputs where you work.** Deals and relationships live in the CRM, generated documents in Document Studio, client-ready quotes and proposals in Quotes, and performance in Analytics.
6. **Make it yours.** Teach the platform your business through Memory, train your team through Classroom, connect your existing tools through Plugins, and style the whole command center in Theme Studio.
